SCOPE OF WORK
Blue Water Capital – CMS Website Development
1. Project Overview
Cayman Webtech will design and develop a professional, responsive, CMS-based website for Blue Water Capital.
The website will present the consultancy’s services, provide educational resources, and introduce a dedicated Business Resource Library where visitors can browse and request business templates, forms, permit-related formats and other professional resources.
The initial resource purchasing functionality will be enquiry-based, allowing users to view the resource and price and submit their information through a form.

8. Resource Enquiry / Purchase Form
For the initial website, resources will use a request-based purchase process rather than a full online checkout system.
The user journey will be:
Browse Resource → View Price → Select Resource → Complete Form → Submit Request
The form will collect relevant customer information, such as:
Name
Email
Phone number
Company/business name
Selected resource
Additional comments/information
The selected resource and applicable price will be included with the submitted enquiry.

21. Initial Resource Purchasing Functionality
The initial website will provide a simple enquiry-based resource purchasing experience.
Users will be able to:
Browse available resources.
View resource information.
View the applicable price.
Submit their information.
Select the resource they wish to purchase/request.
Submit the request to Blue Water Capital for processing.
Full e-commerce, online payment processing and automated digital delivery are outside the initial website functionality.
